# Phase 02 — Domain & Business Architecture

## Purpose

Validate business capabilities, actors, end-to-end and exceptional processes,
bounded contexts, ownership, MVP scope, and authoritative business terminology.

## Planned artifacts

- Capability and bounded-context maps
- As-Is and To-Be process maps
- Actor and responsibility catalogue
- Module capability and data-ownership matrices
- MVP scope and business-rule catalogue

## Entry criteria

- Phase 01 is approved.
- Blocking discovery workshops have named participants.

## Exit criteria

- Business scope and portal phase are explicit.
- Critical UOM, routing, QC, reservation, and fulfillment questions are resolved
  or formally scoped out.
- Cross-domain terminology and ownership are consistent.
- The user explicitly approves Phase 02.
