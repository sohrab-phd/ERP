**OQ-009 — Reusable Residual Threshold**

**Decision:** Residual versus Scrap is determined by an explicit, material/product-family-specific reuse policy. No universal global weight cutoff is assumed.

A leftover qualifies as a **Reusable Residual** when it can be independently identified, stored, and subsequently issued to another production or customer requirement as a valid Inventory Unit.

A leftover that cannot practically be reused as an independently identifiable and issuable material is classified as **Scrap**.

### Operational distinction

**Reusable Residual:**

- remains physically usable,
- can be independently identified,
- can be stored as an Inventory Unit,
- has an authoritative weight,
- can subsequently be reserved/issued,
- retains genealogy to the source material and production operation.

**Scrap:**

- is not retained as reusable inventory,
- is recorded as a scrap movement/fact,
- remains traceable to the production operation,
- does not become available stock unless a later authorized process explicitly converts/recovers it into an inventory-controlled material.

### Threshold policy

The architecture must support residual policies by material/product family.

A policy may define one or more constraints such as:

- minimum weight,
- minimum usable dimensions,
- minimum usable length,
- material/product eligibility,
- other approved physical usability criteria.

The architecture must not define one universal value such as 50 kg or 200 kg for all material families.

Where a threshold policy is not yet defined, the system must not invent one. A residual classification requiring an undefined threshold must remain subject to the appropriate controlled decision/`GUARD_OPEN_POLICY`.

### Residual creation

`CreateResidualUnit` is the authoritative business command for creating reusable residual inventory.

It must:

1. validate the applicable residual policy,
2. create an independently identifiable Inventory Unit,
3. preserve the authoritative weight,
4. preserve genealogy to the source material and production operation,
5. close/split the relevant parent relationship as required,
6. create the corresponding auditable inventory facts.

### Scrap

`PostScrapMovement` is the authoritative command for scrap.

Scrap must remain traceable to its source production operation and material but must not silently become Available inventory.

### Architectural principle

> Residual is reusable inventory with an independent identity; Scrap is a non-reusable production disposition. The distinction is based on explicit physical/business reuse policy rather than an arbitrary global threshold.

**Status:** Architectural baseline accepted. Exact minimum weight, dimensions, and product-family examples remain controlled operational inputs and must be supplied by Production/Inventory/Quality.
