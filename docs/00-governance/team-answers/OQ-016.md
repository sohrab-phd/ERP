**OQ-016 — Recovery Objectives and Retention**

**Decision:** The first-go-live recovery baseline is **RPO ≤ 60 minutes** and **RTO ≤ 8 hours**, supported by daily backups and an off-site backup copy.

### Recovery Point Objective

**RPO: 60 minutes**

The recovery strategy must target a maximum tolerable data-loss window of approximately 60 minutes for authoritative operational data.

The implementation must document which PostgreSQL data and operational artifacts are covered by the RPO.

### Recovery Time Objective

**RTO: 8 hours**

Following a qualifying disaster, the target is to restore the production system to an operational state within 8 hours.

The RTO applies to restoration of the operational ERP service and its authoritative database state, subject to the documented disaster-recovery procedure.

### Backup baseline

The MVP backup baseline includes:

- daily PostgreSQL/database backups,
- an off-site copy of backups,
- protected backup storage,
- documented backup ownership,
- monitoring/verification of backup completion.

The exact backup technology and storage provider remain implementation decisions subject to the deployment environment.

### Restore verification

Backups must not be considered valid solely because a backup job completed successfully.

The project must periodically perform restore tests covering:

1. restoration of the PostgreSQL database,
2. integrity verification,
3. application startup against the restored database,
4. verification of critical Ledger/Balance/Genealogy consistency,
5. measurement of actual recovery time.

Restore-test results must be retained as operational evidence.

### Retention

Exact backup retention duration remains subject to applicable legal, contractual, security, and operational requirements.

The architecture must support configurable retention without embedding an arbitrary permanent retention period.

### Disaster-recovery principle

The authoritative source remains the PostgreSQL transactional data and its durable records.

Recovery must restore the authoritative database state rather than attempting to reconstruct business truth from UI caches, WebSocket messages, temporary files, or derived projections.

### MVP scale alignment

The selected RPO/RTO baseline is intended for the first-go-live modest-scale deployment and does not require Kubernetes, distributed brokers, Redis, or multi-region infrastructure by default.

If future business requirements demand materially stronger availability or recovery guarantees, the infrastructure architecture must be re-evaluated explicitly.

**Status:** Accepted as the recommended first-go-live recovery baseline.
