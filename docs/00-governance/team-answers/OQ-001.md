**OQ-001 — Authoritative UOM Matrix**

**Decision:** Adopt weight-first inventory measurement.

For the first go-live, **kilogram (kg) is the authoritative stock UOM for Coil and other raw-material Inventory Units**. Inventory ledger postings must use the authoritative stock quantity in kg.

Length, piece count, bundle count, and other quantities may be recorded and displayed as secondary or commercial quantities where required by operations, labels, quotations, sales documents, or production records. They must not create a second independent inventory truth.

For materials where length is derived from physical characteristics, conversion must be based on an explicit material/product-specific formula using the relevant dimensions, density, and weight. A universal fixed Coil-to-length conversion is not permitted unless explicitly defined for that material/product.

Where a quantity such as length is physically measured by an approved instrument, the measured value may be retained as an observed measurement and must be distinguished from a calculated conversion.

The system must support explicit UOM conversion rules rather than assuming that all UOMs are universally interchangeable.

**Quantity precision:** Exact decimal arithmetic is required for physical quantities. JavaScript `Number` must not be used as the authoritative representation for weight or monetary calculations. PostgreSQL `NUMERIC` and an appropriate TypeScript decimal representation will be used. The final decimal scale and rounding rule for each UOM remain subject to confirmation from actual plant documents, weighing equipment, and operational practice.

**Recommended initial matrix:**

| Quantity | Role |
|---|---|
| kg | Authoritative stock quantity |
| metre | Secondary/informational or commercial quantity where applicable |
| piece | Secondary/counting quantity where applicable |
| bundle | Packaging/counting quantity where applicable |

**Inventory principle:** There must be exactly one authoritative stock quantity for each Inventory Unit. Secondary quantities are derived or observed measurements and must not independently modify inventory availability or the inventory ledger.

**Status:** Accepted as the architectural baseline, with decimal scale, rounding policy, and material-specific conversion factors to be finalized from first-go-live operational data.

### Coil → Sheet clarification (`2026-09-30`) — measurement only

Factory evidence for Coil → Sheet stock quantity:

- Only measured weight in kilograms is the official stock quantity.
- Count, length, and dimensions are descriptive attributes, not a second
  stock quantity.
- Weight is recorded with zero decimal places. Smallest scale measurement
  is 1 kg. Rounding is not needed for that measurement.
- Weight↔length conversion may be needed. Formula and factors are not
  defined. Do not invent them.
- Weight↔count conversion is not required.

OQ-001 remains treating. This does not close conversion-factor, formula, or
broader arithmetic-scale questions.

### Measurement clarification (`2026-09-30`) — quantity semantics

Confirms the points above and adds:

- Width is a secondary criterion, together with length, thickness, and
  material/type. None is a stock quantity.
- Do not keep parallel stock ledgers for kg, sheet count, metres, or
  dimensions. Ledger kg remains the only stock quantity. Balance stays a
  projection.
- Sheet count and individual Sheet length are still recorded. They are
  not stock quantity. 6 m and 12 m are examples only.
- Dimensions must be representable. No fixed width, thickness, length, or
  dimensional tolerance is defined.
- If expected or calculated weight differs from measured weight, show or
  report the difference. No automatic tolerance is defined. Do not
  auto-classify that difference as acceptable, unacceptable, process loss,
  Residual, or Scrap. The factory does not define a separate process-loss
  category or a production mass-balance percentage.
- This is not OQ-006 fulfillment tolerance and not the Residual/Scrap
  reusability decision.
- Customer-order quantity stays kg.
- Estimate uses required weight × price per kg. Final amount uses actual
  weighbridge weight plus a cutting service fee. That is quantity wording
  only. It does not change sales, price, or invoice workflows. Weighbridge
  device and protocol stay undefined (OQ-011).

Factory measurement precision is 0 decimal places, 1 kg, and no rounding
needed. That is not a requirement to store 0.1, 0.01, or 0.001 kg, and it
is not a completed technical storage design. OQ-001 stays treating.

the stock quantity. Weight↔count conversion is not required. This intake
