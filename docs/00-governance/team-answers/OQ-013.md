**OQ-013 — Organization and Site Model**

**Decision:** First Go-Live operates with **one legal entity and one principal factory site**.

### Legal entity

The MVP assumes exactly one legal entity.

All first-go-live commercial, inventory, production, purchasing, shipping, and operational transactions belong to this legal entity.

Multi-company operation is outside the first-go-live scope.

### Principal site

The MVP assumes one principal factory/site.

The site may contain multiple operational warehouses, storage areas, production areas, and other locations as required by the approved warehouse/location model.

For example:

**Principal Site**
- Raw Material Warehouse
- Work-in-Process Area
- Finished Goods Warehouse
- Scrap/Residual Area
- Shipping Area

Multiple warehouses or locations within the same site do not constitute multiple legal entities or multiple sites.

### Customer isolation

Customer isolation remains a separate concern from legal-entity/site isolation.

The first-go-live authorization model isolates customer data between customers but does not introduce multi-company tenancy.

### Future expansion

The architecture must not make future multi-site or multi-company operation technically impossible.

However, adding another legal entity or principal site is an explicit architecture/business decision requiring revalidation of:

- numbering,
- tenancy/isolation,
- warehouse/location ownership,
- authorization,
- accounting ownership,
- reporting,
- deployment/operational boundaries.

A second company or site must not be introduced merely by inserting an additional database row without the required architectural decision.

### First-go-live baseline

**Legal entities: 1**

**Principal factory sites: 1**

**Additional company/site in first cut: No**

**Status:** Accepted as the recommended first-go-live baseline. Future multi-site or multi-company expansion requires an explicit architecture/business decision.
