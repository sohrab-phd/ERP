**OQ-005 — Quality Plans and Release Authority**

**Decision:** Required Quality Gates are mandatory for inventory availability and shipment eligibility. Quality decisions are made by the Quality domain; Inventory applies the resulting stock-state consequences.

### Incoming QC

Where incoming inspection is required, material received through Goods Receipt enters a QC-pending state.

A required incoming QC that has not been completed successfully must prevent the material from becoming available for normal reservation/issue.

The material may remain physically received and identifiable, but it is not Available inventory until the required QC decision is accepted.

### In-Process QC

Where a production routing requires in-process inspection, the applicable production result remains subject to the defined Quality decision.

A failed or unresolved mandatory in-process QC must prevent the affected result from progressing to a state where it can be shipped.

### Final QC

Where final QC is required for a product family, the product must not become Ship-Eligible until the required final QC has been accepted.

### Quality decisions

The following roles are recommended:

- **QC Inspector:** performs inspections and records results.
- **Quality Engineer / QC Supervisor:** may accept, reject, or quarantine according to the approved Quality Plan.
- **Quality Manager:** may authorize Conditional Release and exceptional release.
- **Second Authorized Approver:** required for exceptional release where the normal Quality Hold is being overridden.

Actual human names will be supplied through OQ-019.

### Conditional Release

Conditional Release is an explicit business command, not a UI flag or silent override.

It must record:

- reason
- affected quality object
- releasing person
- timestamp
- conditions/restrictions
- applicable authorization
- audit information

The release must remain fully auditable.

### Exceptional release

Exceptional release of a Quality Hold requires an explicitly authorized Quality role and a second distinct authorized person.

Shipping and ordinary Inventory users must not independently override a Quality Hold.

### Quality Plan configuration

The system must support product/material-family-specific Quality Plans containing, as applicable:

- incoming checks
- in-process checks
- final checks
- measurement/test definitions
- required/optional status
- sampling requirements
- acceptance criteria
- hold/release behavior

Actual first-go-live checklists, limits, sample sizes, and test methods are operational Quality inputs and must be supplied by the Quality function rather than invented by the architecture.

### Architectural rules

1. Required incoming QC may block inventory availability.
2. Required in-process/final QC may block shipment eligibility.
3. Quality issues the quality decision; Inventory applies inventory-state consequences.
4. No ordinary operational role may silently bypass a Quality Hold.
5. Exceptional release must be explicit, authorized, and auditable.
6. Quality records and inventory ledger facts must not be silently rewritten.

**Status:** Architectural baseline accepted. First-go-live Quality Plans, exact checks, limits, sample sizes, and named approval participants remain controlled business inputs.
