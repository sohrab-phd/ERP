**OQ-002 — Coil Quantity Semantics**

**Decision: A — Weight is authoritative.**

For a Coil, the authoritative inventory quantity is the **actual measured weight in kilograms (kg)**.

Length may be stored as an optional secondary measurement or calculated value for operational, labeling, specification, customer-document, or reporting purposes, but length is not a second authoritative inventory quantity.

The following operations must use the authoritative weight quantity:

- Inventory reservation
- Inventory allocation
- Inventory issue
- Production consumption
- Residual material
- Scrap
- Inventory balance
- Mass-balance validation

The Inventory Ledger therefore remains weight-based.

If a Coil does not have a length measurement, it remains a valid inventory unit as long as its authoritative weight and required identification/evidence are present.

Where length is available, the system should distinguish between:

1. **Measured length** — directly obtained from an approved measurement source.
2. **Calculated length** — derived from weight, density, width, thickness, and the applicable conversion formula.

Neither measured nor calculated length may independently modify the authoritative inventory balance.

**Example:**

Coil C-00125:
- Authoritative weight: 12,480.500 kg
- Optional measured length: 1,842.7 m

The inventory quantity of C-00125 is **12,480.500 kg**.

A reservation for 2,000 kg consumes 2,000 kg of available quantity regardless of whether a length value is present.

**Architectural invariant:**

> A Coil has exactly one authoritative inventory quantity: measured weight in kg. Length is non-authoritative and may be measured or calculated.

**Status:** Accepted as the architectural baseline, subject to confirmation by Inventory/Warehouse and Production against actual shop-floor practice and scale/measurement tickets.
