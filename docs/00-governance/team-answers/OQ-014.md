**OQ-014 — Data and Transaction Volumes**

**Decision:** First Go-Live is planned as a modest-scale deployment. Until reliable 12-month operational counts are supplied, no artificial monthly transaction-volume figures will be used to freeze physical database design, indexes, storage sizing, or hardware capacity.

### Initial capacity planning baseline

The first-go-live planning assumption is:

- **Total users:** fewer than 100
- **Expected concurrent users:** approximately 15–25
- **Deployment model:** single principal site
- **Primary database:** PostgreSQL
- **Architecture:** Modular Monolith with PostgreSQL-backed transactional persistence

These values are planning assumptions, not measured operational limits.

### Transaction volumes

The following monthly figures remain **TBD pending operational evidence**:

- Goods Receipts
- Raw-material / Coil Inventory Units
- Sales Order Lines
- Production Operations
- Inventory Ledger rows
- Shipments
- Other high-volume operational transactions

The architecture must not invent million-row-per-month assumptions merely to justify physical database sizing.

### Physical design

The following remain deferred until actual volume evidence is available:

- final index inventory
- storage sizing
- retention duration based on volume
- hardware sizing beyond the initial deployment baseline
- long-term partitioning decisions
- performance capacity limits

Logical Ledger, Balance, and Genealogy design remains valid independently of these unresolved physical-volume figures.

### Measurement plan

Before production capacity is frozen, the project should collect at least representative historical or signed estimates for:

1. monthly Goods Receipts,
2. monthly Coil/Inventory Units,
3. monthly Sales Order Lines,
4. monthly Production Operations,
5. monthly Ledger rows,
6. monthly Shipments,
7. peak concurrent users.

Each figure should identify its source as one of:

- existing system data,
- operational records/Excel,
- or management estimate.

Where an estimate is used, the responsible operational/sponsor role should approve it.

### Architectural principle

> Logical architecture must not be distorted by invented physical-volume assumptions. Capacity and physical optimization will be evidence-driven after representative operational volumes are available.

**Status:** Accepted as the first-go-live planning baseline. Exact 12-month transaction counts remain operational inputs and are not fabricated by the architecture.
