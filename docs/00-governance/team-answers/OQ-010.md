**OQ-010 — Customer Portal Phase**

**Decision:** MVP Customer Portal = **Option 2 — Visibility Only**.

The first live system may provide a read-only customer portal, but the portal must not create customer orders, reservations, or other binding commercial demand.

### MVP portal capabilities

The portal may provide authenticated customer users with controlled visibility into:

- Sales Order status
- fulfillment/shipment status
- customer-facing documents
- invoice status/documents where authorized
- other explicitly approved customer-facing information

All portal reads must enforce strict customer isolation.

### Explicitly excluded from MVP

The following are not part of the first-go-live portal:

- `PortalPlaceOrder`
- customer-created Sales Orders
- customer-created reservations
- customer-created inventory allocations
- any second Sales write path

`ADP-PORTAL` order-write remains blocked by `GUARD_PORTAL_MVP`.

### Price visibility

Customer-specific prices are **not exposed by default** in the MVP portal.

Price visibility may be introduced in a later phase through an explicit commercial and authorization policy that defines which price, contract, discount, and validity information a customer is permitted to see.

### Traceability visibility

Internal genealogy and production information are not exposed wholesale to customers.

A customer-facing traceability view may be introduced only through an explicitly authorized, customer-safe projection that prevents disclosure of internal suppliers, costs, operational information, or other confidential data.

### Later roadmap

The portal should be designed so that future capabilities can be added without creating a second source of truth.

Potential later phases may include:

1. Customer inquiry/request
2. Customer-specific price visibility
3. Customer order submission
4. Customer-facing traceability

Customer order submission must remain deferred until internal purchase-to-delivery, customer isolation, authorization, and commercial controls have been proven in production.

### Architectural principle

> The Customer Portal is a controlled read channel in MVP, not an alternative Sales transaction boundary.

The internal ERP remains the authoritative source of Sales, Inventory, Production, Quality, Shipping, and Finance facts.

**Status:** Accepted as the recommended MVP baseline. Exact customer-facing document and visibility policy remains subject to commercial/security approval.
