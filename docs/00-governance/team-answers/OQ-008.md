**OQ-008 — Reservation Policy**

**Decision:** A Coil may have only one active reservation at a time. Confirmed-order reservations do not automatically expire while the underlying customer commitment remains valid.

### One-Coil-to-many-orders

A single Coil / Inventory Unit must not be actively reserved by multiple Sales Orders simultaneously.

The authoritative reservation relationship is:

**one Inventory Unit → one active reservation**

No silent reservation splitting across multiple Sales Orders is permitted.

### Conflict resolution

If two confirmed Sales Orders compete for the same Coil, the reservation belongs to the Sales Order with the earlier confirmed timestamp.

The later Sales Order must not steal or silently override the earlier reservation.

If the later order cannot be fulfilled from available inventory, the system records the appropriate Unfulfilled Demand / Must-Buy-or-Make outcome according to the existing feasibility workflow.

### Under-consumption

When actual production consumption is lower than the reserved quantity:

- the unused quantity is released back to Available inventory when it remains part of the same Inventory Unit; or
- `CreateResidualUnit` is used when the remaining material becomes a separately identifiable residual Inventory Unit.

A residual must not retain a hidden reservation from the original order.

### Reservation expiry

A reservation associated with a confirmed Sales Order does **not** automatically expire after an arbitrary number of hours.

It remains active until one of the following occurs:

- fulfillment/consumption completes the relevant commitment,
- the reservation is explicitly released,
- the Sales Order is cancelled,
- the remaining demand is formally recorded as Unfulfilled,
- or an explicitly authorized business exception releases it.

The system must not use an arbitrary global value such as 24 or 72 hours to expire valid confirmed-order reservations.

### ReservationExpirySweep

`ReservationExpirySweep` remains available as a reconciliation and cleanup mechanism for:

- orphaned reservations,
- invalid/stale reservations,
- reservations whose owning business commitment no longer exists,
- or future reservation types that explicitly define a TTL.

It must not invent or impose an expiry period on confirmed Sales Order reservations.

### Future temporary reservations

If the system later introduces temporary planning/availability holds, those reservation types may define an explicit TTL and expiry policy independently of confirmed Sales Order reservations.

### Architectural invariants

1. One Coil has at most one active reservation.
2. Reservation is distinct from Allocation and Consumption.
3. Earlier confirmed Sales Order wins a reservation conflict.
4. A valid confirmed-order reservation cannot be silently stolen by another order.
5. Under-consumption returns unused quantity to availability or creates an explicit residual Inventory Unit.
6. Confirmed-order reservations do not automatically expire based on an arbitrary timer.

**Status:** Accepted as the recommended baseline. Any future temporary reservation type must define its own explicit expiry policy.
