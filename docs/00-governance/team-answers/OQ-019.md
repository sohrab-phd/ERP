**OQ-019 — Named Workshop and Sign-off Participants**

**Decision:** The project will use a role-based workshop and sign-off RACI. Actual human names are not invented by the architecture and must be supplied by the project sponsor/organization.

### Required workshop/sign-off roles

The following roles should participate in requirements validation and/or formal sign-off according to their area of responsibility:

1. **Project Sponsor / Management**
   - Overall business sponsorship
   - Scope and priority decisions
   - Final business sign-off

2. **Project Manager / Product Owner**
   - Requirements coordination
   - Decision tracking
   - Backlog and acceptance coordination

3. **Operations / Factory Representative**
   - Validation of end-to-end factory workflows
   - Operational rules and exceptions

4. **Inventory / Warehouse Manager**
   - Inventory ownership
   - Warehouse/location processes
   - Opening-stock cutover
   - Inventory controls and reconciliation

5. **Production Representative**
   - Production execution
   - Consumption/output
   - Mass-balance and residual workflows

6. **Sales / Commercial Representative**
   - Customers
   - Sales Orders
   - Commercial workflow
   - Customer Portal requirements

7. **Procurement Representative**
   - Suppliers
   - Purchase workflow
   - Receiving workflow

8. **Quality Manager / QC Supervisor**
   - Inspection
   - Acceptance/rejection
   - Quarantine
   - Release and exceptional-release controls

9. **Finance Representative**
   - Finance-Lite requirements
   - Invoice/payment workflows
   - Legal-accounting boundary

10. **IT / Infrastructure Representative**
    - Deployment
    - Backup/recovery
    - Network and operational support

11. **Security / IAM Representative**
    - Authentication
    - Authorization
    - RBAC/policies
    - Audit/security controls

12. **ERP Architecture / Technical Lead**
    - Architecture decisions
    - ADR ownership
    - Technical feasibility and implementation governance

### Separation of duties

The workshop and sign-off model must preserve separation of duties for security-sensitive and financially/materially significant operations.

In particular:

- A person who performs an operational transaction should not automatically be the sole approver of that transaction.
- Inventory adjustments/cutover require appropriate independent authorization.
- Quality exceptional release requires the defined approval chain.
- Security/IAM administration must be separated from ordinary business operation where appropriate.

### RACI ownership

Each major architecture/business decision should identify:

- **Responsible** — performs the work,
- **Accountable** — owns the decision,
- **Consulted** — provides required domain input,
- **Informed** — receives the decision.

The final RACI matrix should be maintained as a project-controlled artifact rather than embedded only in conversational notes.

### Sign-off rule

A decision is considered organizationally approved only when the designated accountable authority has formally signed off through the project's controlled decision/approval mechanism.

Chat-based acceptance is not itself the authoritative organizational sign-off.
