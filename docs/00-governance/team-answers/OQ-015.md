**OQ-015 — Opening-Stock Cutover**

**Decision:** The text below is the recommended architecture baseline for opening stock. It is not a factory-confirmed procedure. In that baseline, opening stock is established through a controlled physical-count and reconciliation procedure, and the approved result is imported as Inventory Ledger facts through `ADP-CUTOVER / OpeningStockImport`. The factory has not confirmed a file, a freeze, signers, or this command.

### Source

The first-go-live opening-stock source will be:

1. controlled physical stock count,
2. supported by an approved cutover/import worksheet where required,
3. reconciled against any available pre-go-live inventory records.

The worksheet/Excel file is a temporary cutover input only. It is not an alternative source of truth after Go-Live.

The exact source files and historical systems used for reconciliation remain to be identified by Inventory/Operations.

### Movement freeze

Before the final physical count:

1. a formal cutover time is announced,
2. normal Inventory movements are frozen,
3. Goods Receipt, Issue, Transfer, Production posting, Shipment, and other stock-changing operations are blocked or controlled,
4. the responsible Inventory authority confirms the freeze.

No unrecorded physical movement is permitted during the final count window.

### Count and reconciliation

The procedure is:

**Movement Freeze → Physical Count → Reconciliation → Discrepancy List → Investigation/Resolution → Named Sign-off → OpeningStockImport**

Any discrepancy between expected/system quantity and physical quantity must be recorded and investigated before the final opening-stock dataset is approved.

Discrepancies must not be silently absorbed into the opening import.

### Sign-off roles

Opening stock may be loaded only after approval by the designated:

- Inventory/Warehouse authority,
- Production/Operations representative where applicable,
- Project Sponsor/Management authority,
- Security/IT cutover authority where required.

Actual human names are deferred to OQ-019.

### Authoritative loading

The approved opening-stock dataset is loaded through:

`ADP-CUTOVER → OpeningStockImport`

Opening stock is recorded as **Ledger facts**.

A Balance-only opening row is prohibited.

`AdjustBalance` is not used as a substitute for opening-stock cutover.

### Rebuild

After the opening Ledger facts are committed:

**Ledger → BalanceRebuild**

and

**Ledger → GenealogyRebuild**

are executed/verified.

Balance and Genealogy therefore remain rebuildable projections of authoritative Ledger facts.

### Post-cutover verification

Before Go-Live is declared successful:

- imported stock must reconcile to the approved signed dataset,
- Ledger totals must reconcile to the signed opening-stock report,
- Balance must reconcile to Ledger,
- applicable Genealogy must rebuild successfully,
- no unauthorized movement may have occurred during the freeze/import window.

### Architectural principle

> Opening stock is a controlled historical fact introduced into the same authoritative Ledger model used by live operations. Excel is a cutover input, never a second operational truth.

**Status:** Accepted as the recommended cutover baseline, not as factory procedure. Exact source files, cutover date/time, discrepancy tolerance/workflow, and named signatories remain operational inputs.
