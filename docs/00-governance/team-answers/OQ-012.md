**OQ-012 — Legal Accounting Integration**

**Decision:** The MVP uses Finance-Lite as an operational financial subledger. A Legal General Ledger/accounting system is outside the MVP implementation scope, but the architecture must preserve a clean integration boundary for a future legal-accounting integration.

### MVP Finance-Lite scope

Finance-Lite remains responsible for operational financial records including:

- Invoices
- Payments
- Payment allocations
- Outstanding balances
- Customer credit-related operational controls
- Financial audit history

Finance-Lite must not be represented as a statutory/legal accounting system.

### Legal accounting

The following are outside the MVP scope unless explicitly approved later:

- Statutory General Ledger
- Full Chart of Accounts
- Statutory financial statements
- Tax accounting
- Statutory accounting reports
- Legal-accounting journal ownership

The system must not invent a legal-accounting integration target or protocol before the company's actual accounting system is identified.

### Future integration boundary

The architecture must maintain an explicit integration boundary through which approved financial facts can later be exported or synchronized to the company's Legal GL/accounting system.

The future flow is conceptually:

**Operational Domains → Finance-Lite → Legal Accounting Integration Adapter → Legal GL**

The Legal GL must not become a direct dependency of Inventory, Sales, Shipping, or Production.

### Source-of-truth principle

Finance-Lite owns its operational invoice/payment facts.

If a future Legal GL integration is introduced, the integration must preserve the authoritative ownership of each financial fact and must not silently rewrite operational records.

Integration failures must be observable, retryable, and auditable.

### MVP behavior

No Legal GL integration is required for MVP.

Any accounting-system-specific configuration, protocol, account mapping, tax mapping, journal mapping, or synchronization contract remains blocked behind `GUARD_OPEN_POLICY` until the actual legal accounting system and its requirements are confirmed.

**Status:** Accepted as the recommended MVP baseline. Finance-Lite is operational only; Legal GL integration is architecturally prepared but deferred.
