**OQ-007 — Sales Order Closure Rule**

**Decision:** A Sales Order is closed when its fulfillment obligation has been completely resolved, independently of invoice payment status.

A Sales Order may move to `CLOSED` when:

1. All ordered demand has been fulfilled/delivered; or
2. The remaining unfulfilled demand has been explicitly cancelled or otherwise recorded as an authorized `UNFULFILLED` outcome.

Partial delivery by itself does not close a Sales Order while an outstanding demand remains.

### Payment independence

Sales Order closure is **not dependent on invoice payment**.

Invoice and Payment have their own financial lifecycle.

For example:

```text
Sales Order:
    CLOSED

Invoice:
    PARTIALLY_PAID
```

is a valid state.

A customer may therefore have received all goods while an invoice remains outstanding.

Likewise, payment of an invoice must not by itself close a Sales Order if fulfillment obligations remain unresolved.

### Domain responsibility

- Sales owns Sales Order lifecycle and fulfillment completion.
- Shipping/Inventory provide fulfillment facts.
- Finance-Lite owns Invoice and Payment lifecycle.
- Sales must not write or manipulate payment state to close an order.

### Closure invariant

A Sales Order must not be closed while it has unresolved demand.

The system must require the remaining demand to be either:

- fulfilled/delivered, or
- explicitly cancelled/unfulfilled through an authorized business action.

### Auditability

When a Sales Order is closed, the system must retain the reason and the fulfillment/unfulfilled quantities that resulted in closure.

**Status:** Accepted as the recommended commercial/architectural baseline. Exact commercial exceptions, if any, may be defined later without coupling Sales Order closure to payment.

### Factory payment methods (`2026-09-30`)

Deposit with remainder after delivery, cheque or promissory note, and
known-customer credit do not change this rule. "After delivery" does not
define a delivery process and does not make `DELIVERED` a close guard.
Invoice issued, payment received, and Sales Order closed stay separate.
OQ-007 stays answered.

The factory has not named who may amend or cancel a customer order.
Mr. Dinavand's production stop/cancel decision is not that authority.
The close rule is not reopened.

The factory has not said that any of the three payment methods is
required before shipment. Who records a payment, invoice linkage,
partial payment, cheque or note settlement, customer balance, and
payment reversal are not this close rule. OQ-007 stays answered.
